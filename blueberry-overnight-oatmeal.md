# Blueberry Overnight Oatmeal
## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup whole rolled oats  
- 1/3 cup milk (or milk alternative of your choice)  
- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt  
- 1/2 banana, finely diced  
- 1 tablespoon peanut butter  
- 1 teaspoon chia seeds  
- Cinnamon to taste  
- Fresh blueberries  

## Directions

Add all ingredients except berries to a Mason jar and mix thoroughly.  
Refrigerate overnight.  

In the morning, top with fresh blueberries and enjoy.
